GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Need something else? Send us a message and our team will help.

How do I book a trip?+

Choose a trip, select Book Now and send the pre-filled message through WhatsApp. Our team will confirm availability and explain the next steps.

Is my booking confirmed when I send the WhatsApp message?+

No. Sending an enquiry does not confirm a booking. Your booking is confirmed only after our team verifies availability and communicates the confirmation.

Can I organise a private group trip?+

Yes. Contact us with your preferred destination, group size and dates so we can discuss a suitable experience.

What should I bring on a trip?+

What to bring depends on the destination. We will share trip-specific guidance before departure.
