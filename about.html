WHO WE ARE

Every destination has a story. Let’s discover it.

KaBabs Adventures is a Ghanaian travel and tourism brand built around a simple idea: life is better when we explore together. We organise group trips, sightseeing experiences, city tours and recreational adventures that help people step outside their routines and connect with new places and people.

From the first enquiry to the journey home, our aim is to make each experience enjoyable, welcoming and thoughtfully organised.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our mission, vision & values.

01 / MISSION

Bring people together

To create memorable and accessible travel experiences that connect people with Ghana’s destinations, culture and natural beauty.

02 / VISION

Make exploration a way of life

To grow into a trusted travel and tourism brand known for meaningful experiences, dependable service and a strong community.

03 / VALUES

Adventure with purpose

Friendliness, reliability, inclusivity, respect for destinations and a commitment to creating great memories.
